# Acme 2025 Annual Report

## Business Overview

Acme Corporation provides industrial automation software. Revenue grew through 2025.

## Financial Statements

### Revenue

Revenue for FY2024 was $100 million. Revenue for FY2025 was $120 million, representing 20 percent growth.

### Operating Income

Operating income for FY2024 was $20 million. Operating income for FY2025 was $18 million. Operating margin declined because cloud infrastructure and research expenses increased.

### Cash Flow

Free cash flow for FY2025 was $14 million. Cash and cash equivalents were $42 million.

## Risk Factors

### Liquidity Risk

The company believes existing cash and operating cash flow are sufficient for at least twelve months. The revolving credit facility provides an additional $30 million.
